# Fortius Inmuebles

## Register
Brand for the public website; product for the separate administration surface.

## Users and purpose
Property owners and buyers in Tijuana and Baja California use the public website to explore properties and contact the firm. The authenticated panel maintains the same product's real data.

## Existing identity
Architectural MNT symbol, Archivo typography, green accents and light/dark themes. Preserve the approved layout and visual identity described in brand.md.

## Design principles
Direct, documented and clear communication. Prioritize WhatsApp and Messenger conversations with confirmed business destinations; forms are an alternative. Never invent contact details or present demonstration properties as real offers. Preserve keyboard access, readable contrast, reduced motion and responsive layouts. Avoid cross-product navigation and fake persistence.
